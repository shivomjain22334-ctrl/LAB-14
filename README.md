### LAB 14 – Capstone Portfolio Presentation

In Lab 14, I learned how to combine the major Generative AI skills from the course into an end-to-end aviation management workflow. The capstone focused on reducing passenger congestion and communication delays during airport disruption events.

The workflow included identifying the business problem, selecting suitable AI tools, designing structured and privacy-safe prompts, generating passenger communication and visual assets, conducting human review, verifying information through official documentation, and producing final portfolio-ready outputs.

The project combined ChatGPT for text generation, Canva Magic Studio for visual assets, and Gemini for RAG-based verification. During the review process, unverified voucher promises, passenger passport information, and a hallucinated 85% delay-reduction statistic were identified and corrected.

The final workflow demonstrated that AI can improve the speed of aviation communication, but human supervision, privacy protection, structured prompting, and source verification remain essential. This capstone helped me understand how different AI tools and techniques can work together to create safer and more professional aviation-management solutions.
